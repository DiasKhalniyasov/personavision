Eye tracker

Records where you look on screenshots of a site, using your own webcam, on your own machine.

Nothing is uploaded. The camera image, the gaze estimate and the screenshots you choose stay in this browser tab. This page makes no network request to any host — it has no code that could — and the recording only leaves the tab when you press Save the file at the end and choose where to put it.

Step 1 of 4

What is being recorded

Gaze is recorded over screenshots, shown one at a time at a fixed size — not over a live site. A page belonging to somebody else cannot be instrumented from a tool like this, and a tool that said it could would be lying about what it measured.

Step 2 of 4

Sit square to the camera

Put your face inside the box, with the room lit from in front rather than behind you. Then keep your head still: this estimates gaze from the image of your eyes, so moving your head undoes the calibration.

Accuracy

How far off the estimate is

A webcam eye tracker is accurate to around 100 px at best. This figure is recorded in the file, so nobody reading the data later has to guess how good the calibration was.

What to do with it

It holds packages/calibration's raw records under .records, so the project reads it with one call: 

import { convertRecords } from '@personavision/calibration';

const file = JSON.parse(await readFile(path, 'utf8'));
const { sessions, rejections } = convertRecords(file.records);

Each record also carries the raw gaze samples the fixations were read from, under gaze, which the converter ignores. README.md beside this page says what every field means.
